Cultural vibe in Russia

Russia has a different rhythm from South Asia. People may look serious at first, but many are helpful when you speak politely and clearly. Learn basic Russian words, respect personal space, and be ready for winter, documents and public rules.

Communication style

Russians often speak directly. It does not always mean they are angry. Be polite, clear and calm.

Russian food and available food types

You can find Russian food, Central Asian food, Caucasus food, European food and many international options. In big cities, halal and vegetarian options are easier to find.

Common Russian food

  • Борщ — beetroot soup
  • Пельмени — dumplings
  • Блины — pancakes
  • Каша — porridge
  • Салат Оливье — Russian salad
  • Котлета — cutlet

Food you can usually find

  • Halal food in many big cities
  • Indian / Pakistani / Bangladeshi restaurants in major cities
  • Uzbek plov, samsa and lagman
  • Georgian khachapuri and khinkali
  • Fast food, pizza, sushi and burgers
  • Vegetarian food, but check ingredients carefully

University hostel life

Hostel rules are different in every university, but most Russian dormitories have common rules. Always read your dormitory contract and ask your dormitory administration.

What you can usually do

  • Live in your assigned room
  • Use shared kitchen, laundry and study area
  • Invite guests if dormitory rules allow it
  • Decorate your space without damaging property
  • Cook simple food in allowed kitchen areas
  • Ask administration for repair problems

What you usually cannot do

  • Break quiet hours at night
  • Damage furniture or university property
  • Smoke in forbidden areas
  • Use dangerous electrical devices if prohibited
  • Bring unknown people without permission
  • Leave garbage or dirty kitchen after cooking

Important hostel rules

Documents

Keep passport, migration card, registration and student ID safe. Do not give original documents to random people.

Roommates

Respect other students’ sleep, prayer, study time and personal space.

Cleanliness

Clean your part of the room, kitchen and bathroom. Hostel problems usually start from dirty shared areas.

РВПО — Temporary Residence Permit for Education

РВПО is a temporary residence permit for educational purposes. It is important for many full-time foreign students in Russia.

Who can usually apply?

  • Foreign students studying full-time in eligible Russian state educational or scientific organizations
  • Usually bachelor, specialist, master, postgraduate, residency and similar full-time programs
  • Preparatory course and online-only students may not be eligible

Why it is useful

  • Gives a stronger legal status during study
  • Can help later with long-term residence steps
  • Reduces some repeated visa pressure for eligible students

Always ask your university international office first because document list and local process can depend on city and university.

ВНЖ — Residence Permit

ВНЖ is a residence permit. It is usually a bigger step after temporary legal status or special eligibility.

Why students think about ВНЖ

  • Longer stay in Russia
  • More stable legal status
  • Future work and life planning
  • Possible step before citizenship for some people

Common idea

Many foreigners first receive temporary status, then later apply for residence permit if they meet legal conditions. Students with РВПО may have a student-related route after graduation, but exact conditions should be checked before applying.

Russian red passport — citizenship

The Russian red passport means Russian citizenship. It is not automatic after study. Foreigners need a legal basis and must follow the citizenship procedure.

General path idea

  • Legal stay in Russia
  • Valid documents and registration
  • Possible РВП / РВПО / ВНЖ route depending on situation
  • Meeting citizenship conditions
  • Application through official procedure

Citizenship rules are legal matters. Never rely only on social media advice. Check official information or a qualified migration lawyer.

Russian rules for illegal foreigners

Do not overstay and do not ignore registration or visa problems. Migration violations can create serious consequences.

What can create problems?

  • Expired visa or migration card
  • No valid registration
  • Working without legal permission
  • False documents
  • Ignoring МВД or university migration instructions

Possible consequences

  • Fine
  • Exit requirement
  • Deportation or expulsion in serious cases
  • Entry ban
  • Problems with future visa, РВПО, ВНЖ or citizenship

СНИЛС and ИНН

These two numbers are very useful for foreigners living, studying or working in Russia.

СНИЛС

СНИЛС is an individual insurance account number. It is often needed for Госуслуги, SIM card procedures, work and some official services.

  • Usually needs passport
  • Foreigners may need notarized Russian translation of passport
  • Can be connected with Госуслуги account

ИНН

ИНН is a taxpayer identification number. It can be useful for work, banking, tax matters and official forms.

  • Issued through tax service procedure
  • Foreigners may need passport and proof of legal stay
  • Documents may need Russian translation

Russian bank rules for foreign students

A Russian bank card is useful for transport, stores, online shopping, salary, scholarship and rent payments.

Usually needed

  • Passport
  • Notarized translation, if bank asks
  • Migration card
  • Registration
  • Russian phone number
  • Sometimes ИНН or СНИЛС

Student tips

  • Use a well-known bank
  • Ask about fees and card service cost
  • Do not share card PIN or SMS codes
  • Be careful with online scams
  • Keep bank app and phone number secure

Russian SIM card and mobile rules

SIM card rules for foreigners became stricter. Prepare documents before buying or confirming a Russian SIM card.

Usually needed for a SIM card

  • Passport
  • Notarized Russian translation of passport
  • СНИЛС
  • Confirmed Госуслуги account
  • Biometric registration through an approved bank or system

Mobile safety rules

  • Do not buy SIM cards from random people
  • Use official operator stores
  • Keep your number registered correctly
  • Do not share SMS codes
  • Use your phone number carefully for bank and Госуслуги
