Top 10 Russian Cities for Students

Here are 10 strong Russian cities for international students. Some cities are better for study, some are better for foreigners, and some give a good balance between education, life and cost.

Best for students

Tomsk

Tomsk is a strong student city with a serious academic atmosphere and many students.

Good for foreigners

Moscow

Moscow is easier for many foreigners because it has more services, transport, universities and international communities.

Best balance

Kazan

Kazan is multicultural, student-friendly and usually more affordable than Moscow or Saint Petersburg.
